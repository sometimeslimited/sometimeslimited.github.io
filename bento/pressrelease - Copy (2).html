Bento Blocks

Press Kit

A cosy puzzle game where you slice and arrange ingredients into bento lunch boxes under a limited cut count.

Description

Bento Blocks is a cozy, puzzly cooking game about making bento lunch boxes. Slice ingredients, rearrange them, and fit them into each box using the fewest cuts possible. Slow down, experiment, and enjoy a gentle soundtrack and soft, hand-painted art as you complete a growing recipe book.

Press Release

Press release date: 23 January 2025

Bento Blocks Launches on January 29th, 2026 — Get Ready to Slice, Arrange, and Perfect Your Lunch Box.

SOMETIMES LIMITED invites players to unwind and sharpen their culinary creativity in this delightful and cozy puzzle game, coming to PC on Steam.

Release Date Trailer

Auckland, New Zealand – 10 December 2025 – SOMETIMES LIMITED is thrilled to announce that Bento Blocks, the cozy cooking puzzle game, will officially launch worldwide on 29 January 2026 (10AM Pacific Standard Time).

Bento Blocks invites players to think outside the lunch box: slice, arrange, and fit ingredients to a given design using the fewest cuts possible. Slow down and enjoy the gentle soundtrack and hand-painted art style.

What began as a two-week game jam project made by a team of three has gathered more attention than the team ever imagined, with over 40,000 wishlists and 40 million+ views across short-form media in a year.

Key Information

  • A cozy, puzzly cooking game about making lunch boxes.
