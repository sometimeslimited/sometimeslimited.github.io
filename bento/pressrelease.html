About Bento Blocks

Bento Blocks is a puzzle game that combines culinary creativity with brain-teasing challenges. Slice, arrange, and perfect your bento-making skills as you craft delicious lunch boxes at your own pace.

Features

  • Discover a fresh take on puzzle games as you slice and arrange ingredients to craft bento boxes.
  • Explore new puzzle mechanics and techniques to complete your recipe book.
  • Over 180 hand-crafted levels, each with its own twist and satisfying solution.
  • Relaxing music and calming, appetising visuals.
  • Play at your own pace with no time pressure or timers.

Press Release

23 January 2025

Bento Blocks Launches on January 29th, 2026 — Get Ready to Slice, Arrange, and Perfect Your Lunch Box.

SOMETIMES LIMITED invites players to unwind and sharpen their culinary creativity in this delightful and cozy puzzle game, coming to PC on Steam.

Release Date Trailer

Auckland, New Zealand - December 10th, 2025 - SOMETIMES LIMITED is thrilled to announce that Bento Blocks, the cozy cooking puzzle game will officially launch worldwide on January 29th, 2026 (10AM PACIFIC STANDARD TIME).

Bento Blocks invites players to think outside the lunch-box, slice, arrange and fit ingredients to a given design using the fewest cuts possible. Slow down and enjoy the gentle soundtrack and hand-painted art style.

What began as a 2 weeks game-jam project made by a team of 3, has gathered more audience than we imagined, with over 40,000 wish-lists, and 40 million+ views across short-form media in a year's time.

Key Information

  • A cozy puzzly cooking game about making lunch boxes.
  • No pressure, no timer, play the game at your own pace.
  • Over 180+ quirky levels.
  • Relaxing soundtrack and ambiance, hand-painted art, and hand-crafted levels.
  • Discover new wacky lunch box designs and backgrounds as you complete the recipe book.
  • Release Date: 29th January 2026 (10AM PST).
  • Available on PC via Steam.
  • Game Length: 4–7 hours.
  • Price point: $14.99 USD / €10.00 EUR / JP¥1800.
  • Awards:
    • Winner: The Pavs Student Slice 2025.
    • Shortlisted: SXSW Best Student Game 2025.
