Description

Bento Blocks is a cozy, puzzly cooking game about the meditative art of making bento lunch boxes. Slice ingredients, rearrange them, and fit them into each box using the fewest cuts possible.

Inspired by daily rituals and the satisfaction of a perfect fit, Bento Blocks features over 180 levels, a hand-painted art style, and a calming acoustic soundtrack.

History

What began as a two-week game jam project has grown into a community favorite with over 40,000 wishlists and 40 million views on social media.
