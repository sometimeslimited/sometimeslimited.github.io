Description

Bento Blocks is a cozy, puzzly cooking game about making bento lunch boxes. Slice ingredients, rearrange them, and fit them into each box using the fewest cuts possible. Slow down, experiment, and enjoy a gentle soundtrack and soft, hand-painted art.

Press Release

23 January 2025

Bento Blocks Launches on January 29th, 2026 — Get Ready to Slice, Arrange, and Perfect Your Lunch Box.

Auckland, New Zealand – 10 December 2025 – SOMETIMES LIMITED is thrilled to announce that Bento Blocks, the cozy cooking puzzle game, will officially launch worldwide on 29 January 2026 (10AM PST).

What began as a two-week game jam project made by a team of three has gathered more attention than the team ever imagined, with over 40,000 wishlists and 40 million+ views across short-form media in a year.

Key Information

  • Over 180+ quirky levels.
  • No pressure, no timer – play at your own pace.
  • Relaxing soundtrack and hand-painted art.
